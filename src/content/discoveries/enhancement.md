Film-coupled nanocube cavities enhance the emission rate of embedded emitters more than a thousandfold while keeping the light bright and directional. This work identified the mechanisms behind the enhancement and showed that extreme Purcell enhancement need not come at the cost of brightness or directionality — the combination the group's later single-photon sources depend on.
