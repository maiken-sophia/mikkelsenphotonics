A single colloidal quantum dot in a nanogap cavity emits single photons at 1550 nm with a lifetime of tens of picoseconds — a spontaneous emission rate enhanced up to 10,000-fold, with blinking almost entirely suppressed — at room temperature and made entirely from solution-processed components.
