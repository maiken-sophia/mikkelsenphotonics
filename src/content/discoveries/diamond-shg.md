title: Second-harmonic light from a material with no bulk nonlinearity

Diamond is centrosymmetric and has no bulk second-order nonlinearity. Inside nanogap cavities, a diamond film only 12 nanometers thick becomes a bright source of second-harmonic light, enhanced more than ten-million-fold, while several nonlinear processes run in the same cavity at once — a nanoscale frequency converter in a material with no bulk second-order response.
