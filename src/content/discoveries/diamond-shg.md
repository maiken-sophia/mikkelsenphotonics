---
title: Second-harmonic light from a material that has none
order: 3
citation: "Shen et al., *Nanophotonics* 10, 589 (2021)"
doi: 10.1515/nanoph-2020-0392
tile: Nanophotonics 2021
---
Diamond is centrosymmetric and has no bulk second-order nonlinearity. Inside nanogap cavities, a diamond film only 12 nanometers thick becomes a bright source of second-harmonic light, enhanced ten-million-fold, while several nonlinear processes run in the same cavity at once — a nanoscale frequency converter in a material that should not be one.
