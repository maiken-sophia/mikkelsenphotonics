Pyroelectric detectors are limited by how fast heat can move. By structuring the absorber as a metasurface only nanometers thick, we made a pyroelectric photodetector with sub-nanosecond response and built-in spectral selectivity — and have since pushed the same idea to gigahertz speeds.
