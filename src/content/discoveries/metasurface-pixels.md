imageAlt: A parrot's head reconstructed in false color from micrometer-scale plasmonic metasurface pixels, 2 mm across.
---
Silver nanocube metasurfaces patterned into micrometer-scale pixels, with six resonances spanning the visible and near-infrared, can be combined into 9,261 colors — enough to reconstruct a photograph in absorption at 560 dpi. The bird above is 2 mm across and made entirely of nanoscale cavities.
